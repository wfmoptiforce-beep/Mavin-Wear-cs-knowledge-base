Mavin Wear Refund Terms & Conditions
These terms govern all refund requests. Agents must communicate and confirm these with customers before processing any refund.
01
✅ Eligibility
🎯Refund Eligibility
Refunds only applicable for:
  • Orders paid online through approved methods
  • Cash collected orders
  • Orders paid to BOSTA
Item must be successfully returned, checked & approved from warehouse.
02
💳 Method
🏦Refund Method
Processed through:
  • Bank transfer to valid bank account
  • E-wallet
  • Gift Card
Method selected at submission. Cannot be changed once selected.
03
🚫 Blocked
⛔Unsupported Methods
❌ Cash transfers
❌ InstaPay card
❌ HSBC accounts
❌ Suez Canal Bank
❌ Next Bank
❌ Kuwait Finance House
04
⏱ Timeline
📅Processing Time
  • Reviewed after order cancellation or successful return
  • Up to 7 business days from cancellation or return confirmation
  • Processing date communicated to customer
05
⚠ Failed
❗Failed Transactions
If refund fails:
  • Status marked as "Failed Transaction"
  • Customer contacted for updated details
  • Processing resumes only after valid info received
06
🔁 Duplicate
🔂Duplicate Transactions
  • Excess amounts may be refunded after Finance verification
  • Supporting documentation may be requested
07
🎁 Gift Cards
🎁Gift Card Purchases
  • Prepaid gift cards are non-refundable
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash
  • Exception: where required by law
08
✔ Verification
🔍Verification & Approval
  • Mavin verifies identity, payment ownership & banking details
  • Incomplete info may delay processing
09
🚫 Non-Refundable
🚫Non-Refundable Cases
Refunds NOT issued for:
  • Orders delivered successfully & not eligible for return
  • Gift card purchases
  • Invalid or unverifiable payment info
  • Suspected fraud or policy violations
📌
Agent Note: Always confirm customer has read and agreed to these T&Cs before submitting. Log confirmation in order notes.
